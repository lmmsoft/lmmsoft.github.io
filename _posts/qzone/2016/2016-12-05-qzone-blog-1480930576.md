    - 步骤2：用TSS来制定训练计划
    - - 三种运动每分钟TSS消耗情况
      - - - Swimming = 1 TSS per active minute of swimming, so a 60 minute swim would equal 60 TSS.
        - - Cycling = Slightly less than 1 TSS per minute, so a 60 minute ride might equal 50 TSS.
        - - Running = Slightly more than 1 TSS per minute, so a 60 minute run might equal 65 TSS.
        - 还有几种方案也能预估，稍微麻烦一点
    - peak bike/run fitness 目标fitness值

    - 关于 fitness的增加比例
    - - 〔原图未能从备份恢复〕 \
      - 每周CTL上升10算是比较快的，要慢慢来
      - 最好是每个月上升5-15CTL比较合适
      - 为了一个月增加10 fitness值，并按照3:1来安排强度周和休息周的话：
      - - 强度周应该是 fitness+30TSS/天
        - 保持周就是 fitness/天
        - 恢复周是 fintness-30TSS/天
      - 使用方法
      - - 如果我fitness是80，想增加，在强度周就应该是110TSS每天，这样如果我在计划40KM的ITT的时候，就可以比较好的制度配速了~
        - 目前我fitness19，下周应该用50TSS每天去计划训练，当然周中会比这个少一点，周末会多一点~
    - 关于TSS分配比例
    - - 游骑跑20/50/30
      - 举个例子
      - TSS goal for the week = 550 TSS (550 / (7 days) = 78.57 TSS per day
      - - - Monday: Rest, 0 TSS
        - - Tuesday: Run 1 hr, 60 TSS, and easy spin, 30 TSS
        - - Wednesday – 1 hour bike, 45 TSS, plus swim
        - - Thursday: Run 1 hr, 60 TSS, plus swim
        - - Friday – Bike trainer set, 80 TSS, plus swim
        - - Saturday: 70.3 base training ride, 180 TSS (3 hours at 60 TSS intensity)
        - - Sunday: Long, zone 1 aerobic endurance run, 90 mins, 90 TSS
        - - TSS total for the week = 545 (-5 from goal TSS)
          - Bike TSS for the week = 335 (47.9 TSS per day)
          - Run TSS for the week = 210 (30 TSS per day)
          - TSS avg/day for the week = 77.9 TSS

    - 学习
    - - VI(Variability Index)= NP/POWER
      - - 越接近1说明骑行越平稳，Sanders大铁骑了1.01，一般1.05以内比较平稳，1.1以外比较糟糕
        - 参考
        - - Sanders的大铁 tp 1.01-0.79-254 <http://home.trainingpeaks.com/athlete/workout/6BY2VTM3TCB2IYHGGBZ367MNKQ>
          - 富阳铁三骑行 strava VI 1.13 IF 0.94 TSS 104 trainingpeaks VI 1.05 IF0.95
          - 合肥铁三骑行 strava 1.16-0.76-177 tp 1.05-0.78-196
          - 威海铁三骑行 strava 1.35-0.81-211 tp 1.23-0.82-253
      - IF Intensity Factor = NP/FTP <http://home.trainingpeaks.com/blog/article/normalized-power,-intensity-factor-training-stress>
      - - - Less than 0.75 recovery rides
        - - 0.75-0.85 endurance-paced training rides
        - - 0.85-0.95 tempo rides, aerobic and anaerobic interval workouts (work and rest periods combined), longer (&gt;2.5 h) road races
        - - 0.95-1.05 lactate threshold intervals (work period only), shorter (&lt;2.5 h) road races, criteriums, circuit races, longer (e.g., 40 km) TTs
        - - 1.05-1.15 shorter (e.g., 15 km) TTs, track points race
        - - Greater than 1.15 prologue TT, track pursuit, track miss-and-out
      - TSS <http://home.trainingpeaks.com/blog/article/normalized-power,-intensity-factor-training-stress>
      - - - TSS less than 150 - low (recovery generally complete by following day)
        - - 150-300 - medium (some residual fatigue may be present the next day, but gone by 2nd day)
        - - 300-450 - high (some residual fatigue may be present even after 2 days)
        - - Greater than 450 - very high (residual fatigue lasting several days likely)
